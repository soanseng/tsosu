Tsosu (tsò-sū, Taiwanese for "doing things") is a task manager designed by a psychiatrist with ADHD for people whose brains work differently.

FOCUS 3
Pick just 3 tasks for today. Everything else is bonus. No guilt, no overwhelm.

HABITS WITHOUT SHAME
Track daily habits with flexible streaks. Missed a day? No "streak broken" messages. Just "5 out of 7 days" — because progress isn't perfection.

ENERGY MATCHING
Tag tasks by energy level (high/medium/low). When you're running low, Tsosu suggests tasks that match your current capacity.

PICK ONE
Can't decide what to do? Tap the dice. Tsosu picks a task based on your energy level and available time.

TINY VERSIONS
Every habit has a 2-minute version. "Meditate" becomes "sit and take 3 breaths." Starting is the hardest part.

GENTLE NUDGES
Morning routine reminders that respect your pace. No guilt trips. No anxiety-inducing badge counts.

SYNC WITH YOUR OWN FILES
Everything is stored as plain markdown in a folder you own. Point Tsosu at your Obsidian vault and tasks stay in sync across devices — edit on desktop with Obsidian or nvim, sync with Syncthing or Obsidian Sync. No server required.

CALENDAR INTEGRATION
Sync tasks to Google Calendar or any CalDAV calendar (Fastmail, Nextcloud, etc.). See your tasks alongside your schedule.

WEEKLY REVIEW
Celebrate what you accomplished. Focus on progress, not what's left undone.

TODOIST IMPORT
Switching from Todoist? Import your tasks from CSV.

BILINGUAL
Full English and Traditional Chinese (zh-TW) support.

PRIVACY FIRST
No analytics. No ads. No tracking. Your data lives in your own markdown files.
